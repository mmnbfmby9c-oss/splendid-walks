# Splendid London Walks

A private audio walking companion for *Rather Splendid London Walks*.

- Open on an iPhone in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
- The book's text and photos are **not** in this repository. Each person adds their own from their copy of the book in the app's settings, and it's stored only on their phone.
- Stop summaries and walking directions here are original writing for this app.
